Add X Session Cookies

To search X reliably without automated login challenges, provide the session cookies from an active browser session on x.com.

How to get cookies:
  1. Open x.com in your browser and ensure you are logged in.
  2. Press F12 (or Right Click → Inspect) → Go to Application (or Storage) tab.
  3. Under Cookies, click https://x.com.
  4. Copy the values for auth_token and ct0.
Paste full cookie header or at least auth_token=...; ct0=...
